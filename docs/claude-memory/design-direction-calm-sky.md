The user shared CRED Coin Rush screenshots (again on 28 Sep 2026) as the look they want: painted sky from blue to warm cream, metallic gold serif headlines with one italic phrase, wide-spaced gold capitals for labels, glossy white pills, frosted glass cards, objects shown on a pedestal, calm spacing. This is the Calm Sky direction (Engineering Plan v0.2, section 8A). Take the mood, never CRED's assets or branding.

On 28 Sep 2026 the user said the built app felt boring, static, over-padded, inconsistent, and that dark mode looked bad, but that the website's light colours are very good. The agreed redesign (canvas "MedLearn OS redesign", https://claude.ai/artifact/USY8phzzTdZsvdVKpqbkeX) keeps the light palette and adds: one hero per screen (the next thing to do, with the 3D model on a pedestal), streak, daily ring, plan that ticks off, tighter spacing, 3D tab in the centre of the nav. Dark becomes "night": near-black, dark glass, champagne gold serif, bright teal actions (not navy). Layouts exist for phone, tablet (side rail, docked studio panels) and laptop (rail + main + sidebar, three-pane studio).

Later on 28 Sep 2026 the user said the built site did not match the canvas: too much padding, corners not round or consistent, poor tablet/laptop landscape, and the 3D studio off-design. They judge the site against the canvas side by side, so match it closely rather than loosely.

**Why:** The user wants the app to feel premium, alive and calm, and it has to work on phone, tablet and laptop.
**How to apply:** Build screens from the Calm Sky parts in packages/ui and follow the canvas; one accent (teal) for actions, gold for headlines and labels. Check phone, tablet, laptop and dark mode with screenshots before calling UI work done. Related: [[3d-studio-direction]], [[engineering-standards]], [[medlearn-os-project]]
