female-pelvis.glb

The female pelvis, its organs, ligaments, levator ani and perineal muscles, for the female pelvis
topics in the 3D studio. Each structure is one named mesh, simplified and without textures.

This work is based on "Female Reproductive System"
(https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/c4d5e725198d4a1997c641d548bb20ce) by Stanford Medicine EdTech
(https://sketchfab.com/stanfordedtechteam), licensed under CC BY 4.0
(http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Downloaded 17 October 2026 (GLB, 1k textures).

Changes: the meshes were turned from Y-up metres to Z-up millimetres and moved onto the
BodyParts3D pelvis, renamed, simplified and compressed, and their textures removed
(scripts/models/build-models.mjs, model "female-pelvis").
