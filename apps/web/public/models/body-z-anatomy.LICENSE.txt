body-nerves.glb, body-lymph.glb, and in body-organs.glb the liver and the five lobes of the lungs

The whole body's nervous system (brain, brainstem, spinal cord, cranial and spinal nerves, the
plexuses and the peripheral nerves of the limbs) and its lymph nodes by group (cervical,
axillary, mediastinal, abdominal, pelvic, inguinal and popliteal, with the lymphoid organs), as
layers of the one body in the 3D studio. Each structure is one named mesh.

Source: Z-Anatomy, by Gauthier Kervyn and Lluís Vinent Juanico, built on BodyParts3D
((c) The Database Center for Life Science). FBX set from
https://github.com/LluisV/Z-Anatomy/tree/PC-Version/Resources/Models/FBX
(NervousSystem100.fbx, LymphoidOrgans100.fbx, VisceralSystem100.fbx), downloaded 5 October
2026.
