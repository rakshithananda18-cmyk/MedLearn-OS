shoulder-chest.glb

Bones, muscles, arteries and veins of the right shoulder and chest, shared by the upper limb
topics (each topic shows its own parts): clavicle, scapula, humerus, ribs 1 to 6 with their
costal cartilages, sternum, C5 to T1 vertebrae; pectoralis major and minor, subclavius, serratus
anterior, subscapularis, teres major, coracobrachialis, short head of biceps; subclavian,
axillary (whole, and split into its three parts where pectoralis minor crosses it), brachial,
thoracoacromial branches, lateral thoracic, subscapular, circumflex scapular, thoracodorsal,
anterior and posterior circumflex humeral and internal thoracic arteries; subclavian, axillary,
cephalic and basilic veins.
